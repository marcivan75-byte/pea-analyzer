# AT Weekly Quarterly Study Specification V1

Research branch only: `research/at-weekly-v1-20260829`.

This study starts only after Block E has been validated and LOCKED. It must use the fully validated A-B-C-D-E architecture without re-optimising already locked blocks.

## Scope

Period: from 2023-01-01 to latest validated completed quarter/week available in the research dataset.
Granularity: calendar quarter (Q1/Q2/Q3/Q4) plus consolidated total since 2023-01-01.
Entry model set: the two locked entry models only.
Capital simulation: fixed nominal entry allocation of EUR 5,000 per executed trade. No compounding unless a later explicit decision changes this rule.

## Required quarterly fields

For each calendar quarter report:

1. Potential entry signals: count of valid completed-week entry signals generated by the locked entry models before position/execution constraints.
2. Executed entries: count of actual simulated positions opened. This must be reported separately from potential signals to avoid ambiguity.
3. Early false positives eliminated: exits explicitly classified by the locked early false-positive block.
4. Early invalidations: exits due to protective stop or other non-profit invalidation rules. Break out at least protective-stop exits versus other invalidation exits.
5. Take-profit on reversal: exits classified by Block D confirmed reversal.
6. Take-profit on overheating: exits classified by locked Block E. If an exit contains both D and E evidence, report it once in total exits and also in a separate `D+E joint` diagnostic bucket to prevent double counting.
7. Winning trades: count of closed executed trades with return > 0.
8. Average winning return (%): arithmetic mean of winning-trade returns.
9. Maximum winning return (%): maximum closed-trade return in the quarter.
10. Losing trades: count of closed executed trades with return < 0.
11. Average losing return (%): arithmetic mean of losing-trade returns.
12. Maximum loss (%): minimum closed-trade return in the quarter.
13. Quarterly reward/risk: average winning return / absolute average losing return. Report NA where no valid winner or loser sample exists.
14. Quarterly gross EUR P&L using EUR 5,000 nominal per executed trade: sum(5000 * return_pct / 100) over closed trades allocated to the quarter according to exit date.
15. Quarterly net EUR P&L: gross P&L minus explicitly modelled costs/slippage. If costs are not yet validated, report both `gross` and `net before costs` and do not imply real-world net performance.
16. Capital deployed (EUR): 5,000 × executed entries, reported as turnover/deployed nominal, not as required simultaneous account capital.
17. Return on deployed nominal (%): quarterly EUR P&L / quarterly deployed nominal × 100, with denominator caveat.

## Consolidated section since 2023-01-01

Report all fields above over the full period, plus:
- cumulative EUR P&L at EUR 5,000 per trade;
- total potential signals;
- total executed entries;
- total early false-positive exits;
- total protective-stop / invalidation exits;
- total D take-profit exits;
- total E take-profit exits;
- D+E joint exits;
- total winners / losers and win rate;
- average gain, maximum gain, average loss, maximum loss;
- consolidated reward/risk;
- profit factor;
- P10 return;
- maximum loss;
- endpoint marks separately, excluded from execution counts and EUR realised P&L unless explicitly marked-to-market in a separate non-realised field.

## Attribution rules

- Potential entries are attributed to the quarter of their completed-week signal date.
- Executed entries are attributed to the quarter of actual entry date.
- Exit categories, winners/losers and EUR P&L are attributed to the quarter of actual exit date.
- A trade spanning quarters appears as one entry in its entry quarter and one exit/P&L event in its exit quarter.
- `ENDPOINT_MARK` is never an execution and must not be counted as a realised gain/loss or take-profit.
- Protective stop gap-through uses actual open fill; normal touch uses stop price, per locked stop contract.
- No double counting across exit categories.

## Deliverables

- Machine-readable JSON with one record per quarter and one consolidated record.
- CSV quarterly summary.
- Trade-level audit CSV with signal date, entry date, exit date, entry model, entry price, EUR 5,000 nominal, exit price, exit reason(s), return %, EUR P&L, quarter entry, quarter exit, endpoint flag.
- Concise Markdown/CI summary highlighting quarterly stability, deterioration, outlier quarters and sample-size warnings.

## Validation gates

The quarterly study is valid only if:
- Block E status is LOCKED in the decision registry;
- all A-E locked parameters in output match registry values;
- no-lookahead controls pass;
- protective-stop contract tests pass;
- potential-signal and executed-entry counts are explicitly distinguished;
- exit-category reconciliation holds: realised closed exits = false-positive + stop/other invalidation + D-only + E-only + D+E joint + any explicitly documented residual exit class;
- quarterly winner + loser + zero-return counts reconcile to realised closed exits;
- EUR P&L reconciles exactly to trade-ledger returns × EUR 5,000 nominal;
- endpoint marks do not enter realised P&L.

No quarterly result should be represented as robust where the trade sample is too small; sample sizes must always be shown.
